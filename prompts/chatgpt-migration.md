# Moving from ChatGPT

**Start with Claude's built-in memory import.** In Claude, go to **Settings → Your Claude → Start import** (older layout: Settings → Memory), copy the prompt it gives you into ChatGPT, read the answer, remove anything sensitive, and paste it back. That's enough for most people.

Then copy your ChatGPT custom instructions (ChatGPT → Settings → Personalisation) into **Settings → Your Claude → Instructions**.

**Optional, more detailed export.** If you also want a structured list of your work and home context (custom GPTs included), paste the prompt below into ChatGPT. Read what comes back and delete anything you don't want to carry over. You can paste it into a chat in your Claude Project for reference.

If you use Claude for work too, put work in its own Project so client detail never reaches the family calendar or your partner's weekly email. Check your employer's or clients' rules on using AI with their information first.

```
I'm moving to a new AI assistant and want to bring all my context with me, for both work and home.

List everything you know about me from your memory and our past chats. Plain bullet points, grouped under these headings, no commentary:

1. How I like responses (tone, length, format, things I dislike)
2. About me (background, strengths, how I work best)

WORK
3. My work: employer or business, what I do, types of clients (no names), my working week
4. My working pattern (days and hours I work, where I work, regular commitments)
5. Current projects, priorities and deadlines
6. Recurring work tasks I've asked you to help with (invoices, emails, proposals, reports, planning)
7. Templates, formats or styles I use at work
8. Tools and systems I use

HOME
9. My household (partner, children, pets; first names and ages only)
10. School, nursery and childcare arrangements
11. My partner's commitments (work, sport, social)
12. Regular household jobs and reminders
13. Ongoing personal projects or plans
14. Interests and hobbies

OTHER
15. Any custom GPTs I use, what each one does, and its instructions if you can see them
16. Anything else you know that would help a new assistant help me

Rules:
- Only include things I've actually told you. Don't guess or fill gaps.
- If you're unsure about something, put "(check)" after it.
- Leave out dates of birth, medical details, passwords, and any ID or bank numbers.
- Leave out confidential client details and any income or tax figures. Just name the topic.
```
